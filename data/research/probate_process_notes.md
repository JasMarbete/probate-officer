## Who can apply
- The executors named in a will can apply. Without a will, the closest living relative can apply. (Applying for probate)
- A solicitor or other probate practitioner can apply on your behalf. (Applying for a grant of representation)

## Before applying
- Check whether probate is needed. Some assets, such as savings, shares or jointly held money and property, can pass without it. (Applying for probate)
- Estimate the estate's value. This is needed even when no Inheritance Tax is due. (Before you apply)
- Decide whether the estate is an "excepted estate". If it is not, full details must go to HMRC on form IHT400. (Before you apply)
- Excepted estates can apply straight away. Other estates must report, and wait for HMRC's code if tax is due. (Before you apply)

## Inheritance Tax timing
- Report the estate's value on IHT400 within one year of death, if Inheritance Tax is owed. You cannot apply for probate until this is done. (Reporting IHT)
- Pay Inheritance Tax by the end of the sixth month after death to avoid interest. Payment normally starts before probate is granted. (Reporting IHT)
- If tax is due, HMRC sends a unique code that is needed before applying. (Before you apply)

## Documents
- Death certificate, if the death occurred outside England or Wales, or if an interim coroner's certificate is held. (Before you apply)
- Original will, if there is one. (Before you apply)
- Form IHT400, if full details must be reported. (Before you apply)
- HMRC unique code, if tax is due. (Before you apply)
- Estate value figures from the IHT checker. (Before you apply)

## Forms
- With a will: form PA1P. Without a will: form PA1A. (Grant of representation)
- Online or by post. Postal applications take longer. (Grant of representation)

## Multiple applicants
- Choose a lead applicant. The lead cannot continue until every applicant has signed the legal declaration. (Before you apply)

## Fees
- Estates over £5,000: £526 application fee. Estates of £5,000 or less: no fee. (Probate fees)
- Extra copies: £2 each if ordered with the application, £16 each if ordered later. A second application after a grant is £22. (Probate fees)
- Help with fees is available on low income or certain benefits. Apply before applying for probate, using the online service or form EX160. Help does not cover extra copies. (Probate fees)

## Timescales and other points
- A grant is usually issued within 12 weeks of applying. Longer if more information is needed. Paper applications are slower. (Grant of representation)
- A caveat can be entered to stop an application before a grant, for example in a dispute. (Applying for probate)
- IHT reference figures: £325,000 threshold, or £500,000 where a home goes to direct descendants. Rates 40%, or 36% if 10% or more of the net value goes to charity. (Valuing the estate)
